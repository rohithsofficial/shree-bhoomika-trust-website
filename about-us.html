Our Mission

It is sad to note that so many elderly people's lives are full of miseries and grief. They are not getting daily basic facilities like food, clothes, and accommodation. The Shree Bhoomika Social Service Trust (R) came forward to help those senior citizens who are neither able to earn their daily bread nor have anyone to support them.

We provide free food, clothes, and accommodation to 50 inmates and have been running this noble service for 5 years. The trust is totally run by donations received from generous donors and the management committee. Although registered with the government, we rely entirely on the support of kind-hearted individuals.

How You Can Help

Sponsorship Options: You can sponsor food for one day, one week, or one month. The sponsorship for providing full day food per day is Rs. 2,000/-. Even a small contribution of Rs. 50/- can support one meal for an inmate.

We accept donations in kind or through cash/cheques/DD in favor of Shree Bhoomika Social Service Trust (R).

These neglected senior citizens need your affection, attention, and recognition. Your moral and financial support will enable them to pass the rest of their lives in peace and tranquility. We appeal to all kind-hearted citizens to come forward and donate generously for this noble cause.

Our Commitment

The management committee requests you to come forward and donate generously to meet the growing needs of the home. Every contribution, no matter how small, makes a significant difference in the lives of these abandoned elderly people who deserve dignity, care, and compassion in their golden years.
