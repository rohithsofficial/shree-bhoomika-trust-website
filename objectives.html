Our Mission & Vision

Shree Bhoomika Social Service Trust (R) is committed to comprehensive social welfare through integrated development programs, focusing on rural empowerment, education, healthcare, and sustainable community development across Karnataka.

Detailed Objectives

1

Human Resource Training Centers

To organize and establish human resource training centers in the field of cultural, self-employment, rural development or eradication of social evils and promote national integration.

Education & Training
2

Watershed & Infrastructure Development

To undertake and implement programs relating to watershed rainwater harvest construction of low cost-latrines, roads, small bridges, culverts, soil conservation work, and contrabands in rural areas.

Infrastructure
10

Integrated Development Programs

To implement various integrated development programs in rural and undeveloped urban areas. That is SHG (Self Help Groups) for Men and Women.

Community Development
11

Rural Development Programs

To help encourage and undertake all types of rural development programs.

Rural Development
12

Hostel for SC/ST & Underprivileged

To establish hostel for SC/ST backward and poor and needy.

Education & Shelter
13

Holistic Development

To lay the foundation for proper physical psychosocial development of urban and rural people.

Health & Wellness
14

Government Schemes Implementation

DPAP, DRDA, EAS, NWDPPRA, NSC, MRA & E, IWDP, WA, KVK, Dwcra, WC WCT & all Rural and SC/ST and other OBC programs.

Government Programs
15

Healthcare & Nutrition Programs

Health service, Nutrition programs, Adult Education programs, Dairy Development programs, Emergency relief and income generation programs in Urban and Rural agriculture, sericulture and Horticulture to poor, small and marginal farmers.

Healthcare
16

Children & Women Welfare

To provide home for destitute children, welfare of street children, Rag pickers welfare, and welfare of devadasis.

Child Welfare
17

Women's Vocational Training

To provide basic vocational training to women and young girls.

Women Empowerment
18

Village & Small-Scale Industries

To promote the village and industries small-scale industries and village water supply.

Industry Development
19

Environment & Social Forestry

To bring awareness among the women belonging to all communities about environment, social conservation and ecological imbalance and to bring un-cultivable land into social forestry.

Environment
20

Welfare of Vulnerable Groups

To take all possible activities for the welfare of the disabled, aged, women, youth and children.

Social Welfare
21

Research Activities

To take up research activities in all subjects related to schedule caste/schedule tribe and on social problems.

Research
22

Agriculture & Horticulture

To encourage and develop projects like agriculture, horticulture, waste land development programs, and income generating activities for the poor women in the society.

Agriculture
23

Financial Support & Self-Help

Borrow funds from Rastriya Mahila Vikasa, NABARD, SIDBI, Banks, CAPART, MNGO & RMK finance institutions, Government and Government Bodies to promote self-help and women groups for sustainable development.

Financial Support
24

Development Project Funding

The organization may accept loans and advances from Government Agencies, Banks, corporations, Trusts, Local Bodies, institutions and other agencies to undertake development projects and other schemes approved by the board.

Project Funding
25

Youth Education & National Awareness

Enabling youth to receive education and knowledge about Indian culture, freedom, constitution of India, five year plans, projects on environment, and population control.

Youth Development
26

Grant & Funding Mobilization

To take up and receive funds from the state and central governments or both, or non-government agencies and also abroad agencies in order to provide income generating activities for the poor and the needy.

Funding
27

Low Cost Housing Technology

To establish Nirmithi Kendras to promote low cost building technology and interface with the Housing corporations towards the shelter needs of the common and poor people.

Housing
28

Coconut & Coir Industry Development

To pave the way for growth of the coconut industry through (a) insect pests and disease affected gardens management, (b) Processing & product diversification, (c) Market Research and promotion, and coir industry development.

Industry Development
29

Water Conservation & Infrastructure

To provide afforestation, tank rehabilitation, check dam construction, and related water conservation infrastructure.

Water Conservation
