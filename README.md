# 🌿 Shree Bhoomika Social Service Trust (R)

This is the official website for **Shree Bhoomika Social Service Trust (R)** — a non-profit organization dedicated to supporting senior citizens and promoting social welfare initiatives.

---

## 🏠 Website Overview

The website helps visitors:

- Learn about the Trust’s mission and objectives  
- Explore its activities and community work  
- View gallery photos and trustee details  
- Find legal information and contact details  
- Donate to support the Trust’s efforts  

---

## 📄 Pages Included

- `index` – Home  
- `about-us` – About the Trust  
- `trustees` – Trustees  
- `legal` – Legal Documents  
- `objectives` – Objectives  
- `activities` – Activities  
- `gallery` – Gallery  
- `contact-us` – Contact Information  
- `Donation` – Donation Page  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5**

2. Import it into your Vercel dashboard  
3. Deploy and get your live URL instantly
