Health Development

Providing accessible healthcare services and creating health awareness in rural and urban communities

1

To conduct free medical camps and medical care services to poor and needy in rural and urban areas.

2

To undertake and implement programs relating to create awareness on various issues like health care, children care, AIDS/HIV, sanitation, environment, family planning, social forestry, legal aid co-operation wings, self-help group formation, women empowerment, child labour and social evils etc.

3

To organize health camps, eye camps, sanitation environment programs, ecology development for the rural and urban areas and to serve the society.

4

To take up appropriate health activities for needy people in urban and rural areas.

5

To arrange and provide medical assistance for the needy persons who are incapable financially - SC/ST minorities and other poor classes in villages and the society.

6

To arrange pulse polio programs, RCH, small family norms, accident blood bank and other all programs.

Education Development

Building educational infrastructure and providing quality education to empower communities

1

Establish, maintain and run educational institutions i.e., schools, creches, colleges, technical education, commercial vocational adult, agriculture, musical, ashrams, physical medical, veterinary, physically handicapped education, training institutions, destitute homes, hostels, fine arts, professional educational and any other education institutions under private management or by any other ways best suited to the needs of the people and development of the association.

2

To provide education facilities to deserving students.

3

To take up educational programs and activities for all categories and disabled, through integrated education and through special schools.

4

To conduct study tours to places of cultural importance in or out of state.

5

To start schools for imparting education in Hindi and Sanskrit etc.

6

To open Dental, Medical College from the above said institution.

7

To open training institutions, ITI, Diploma, D-Pharma, D.Ed, CPED, Nursery, Nursing I.E.D, or M.C.C.T & JOC from the institutions.

8

Cultural shows, essay, painting and competitive programs. To open the computer training center.

9

Child labour residential school, day care center, de-addiction center and computer training centers.

10

Shishupalana Kendras, to provide stay homes.

11

To open or commerce LKG, UKG primary High schools, Colleges, PU colleges, post graduate college.

12

To establish, supervise, manage the religious, secular, technical and commerce education institutions.
